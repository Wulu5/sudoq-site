Privacy Policy

SudoQ for iPhone, iPad and Mac · effective 10 October 2026

SudoQ does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code in the app, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and any game in progress are saved in the app's own storage on your device. The widget reads a small summary of that progress (your streak and which days you played) from storage shared only between the app and its widget on the same device.

Optional Apple services

These are Apple's services, used through Apple's own frameworks. Both can be switched off in the app's settings.

  • iCloud sync keeps your progress in step across your devices using your own iCloud account. The data lives in your iCloud account; the developer cannot see it.
  • Game Center records achievements under your Game Center profile. Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

Purchases

The one-time Root Access purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Root Access has been bought.

Reminders and sharing

The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server. When you share a result, the system share sheet sends it only where you choose.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Removing your data

Settings → rm -rf ~/.sudoq resets your progress on this device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
