A daily board for everyone, a career ladder from Script Kiddie to CTO, eleven variants and a console with commands and easter eggs. No ads, no subscription.

Questions

How do I restore Root Access on a new device?

Open Variants, Dev Mode or Ranking. The purchase screen that appears has a restore button. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported.

Why can't I open the next level?

Boards open one at a time: solve a board to open the next one, and finish a level to open the one after it. Anything you have already solved always stays open.

Does replaying a Classic board improve my time?

No. Classic, Dev Mode and Daily boards are fixed, so only your first solve counts. You can replay them for fun. Variants and Ranking runs are generated fresh every time, and every result there counts.

When does the daily board change?

At midnight in your time zone. Everyone gets the same board on the same date.

How do I keep progress in sync across devices?

Turn on iCloud sync in the app's settings on each device, signed in to the same iCloud account.

How do I start over?

Settings → rm -rf ~/.sudoq resets your progress on that device.
